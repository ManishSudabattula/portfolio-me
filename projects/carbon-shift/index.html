The problem

The IEA projects data-centre electricity consumption to more than double by 2030, with AI a major driver. Many evaluations, fine-tunes, and batch inference jobs are flexible enough to shift, but schedulers rarely optimize for carbon, price, and delivery together.

System design

Make compute demand responsive.

01 / OBSERVE

Forecast the signals

Ingest hourly carbon intensity, regional price, renewable share, weather, and confidence for each compute region.

02 / CLASSIFY

Know job flexibility

Tag workloads with runtime, GPU shape, deadline, checkpoint support, data residency, and interruption tolerance.

03 / OPTIMIZE

Score feasible windows

Minimize weighted carbon and cost while enforcing deadline, capacity, location, and forecast-confidence constraints.

04 / VERIFY

Report actual impact

Compare the completed run with an immediate baseline and publish energy, emissions, cost, and SLA deltas.
